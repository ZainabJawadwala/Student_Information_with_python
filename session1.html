Gazanias are also known as treasure flowers or African daisies. Their common names reflect their vibrant, treasure-like blooms and their origin in South Africa.
Gazania species are grown for the brilliant colour of their flowerheads which appear in the late spring and are often in bloom throughout the summer into autumn.
They prefer a sunny position and are tolerant of dryness and poor soils KNOW MORE..
